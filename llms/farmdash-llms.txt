## Summary

FarmDash helps airdrop farmers and autonomous DeFi agents stop farming blind with structured DeFi opportunity intelligence, zero-custody routing, x402 payment flows, and control/session context. It aggregates protocol opportunity data, wallet-visible farming progress, normalized points signals, swap-routing context, and Sybil-risk warnings into one zero-custody platform. The product is built around three surfaces:

- Human dashboard: Track wallets, source-labeled protocol activity, normalized progress, alerts, and protocol exposure. Unknown rewards remain unknown rather than being assigned a promised payout.
- Opportunity intelligence: Compare live airdrop routes using the canonical Trail Heat score (DeFiLlama-backed) and catalog discovery heuristic scores, protocol status, chains, categories, and evidence labels.
- Agent infrastructure: Use Signal Architect, MCP, OpenAPI, x402 payments, and the agent-kit SDK for canonical quantitative Trail Heat data (/api/v1/trail-heat), discovery heuristic scores (catalog), swap quotes, fee history, wallet intelligence, futures research, and autonomous workflow context.

FarmDash is not a broker, custodian, or financial advisor. Airdrops are not guaranteed. Scores are decision-support signals derived from public chain data, protocol APIs, static opportunity metadata, and clearly marked estimates.
